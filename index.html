Authorship and AI

Bill Price

Description

This open educational resource provides modular instructional materials for integrating generative AI responsibly into academic writing courses. Designed for composition instructors, writing program administrators, and students navigating AI-assisted writing workflows, these materials address critical questions about authorship, intellectual integrity, and maintaining agency when working with large language models. Each module can be used independently or sequenced into a complete curriculums. Materials are built for flexibility across institutional contexts and writing curricula, from first-year composition onward.
